Welcome to color race. Simple web game, multi-player. The idea is to make the start pattern match the target pattern in the inner 3x3. Mobile and desktop friendly. Use your mouse or touch to click on tiles. They will slide into the open position (the black tile). Leaderboards are for time and number of moves to solved.

Still in development. Enjoy playing. Leaderboards are not stored forever, puzzles may repeat. The three character inputs available for the leaderboards are restricted to plain ascii A to Z.

Your playthrough and move set will be played back to other people who are solving the same puzzle as you are. So you will grant us a (deep breath) non-exclusive right to store, transfer, display, fold, spindle, mutilate and otherwise do with as we please your play through moves.

Well, that was a mouthfull.
